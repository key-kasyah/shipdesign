## Users

## Product Purpose
An OPART Lab ship-design engineering workspace with deterministic calculations, requirements, preliminary design and geometry editors. AI explanations support engineering work; they are not the source of engineering truth.

## Capabilities and Constraints
UI-only reconstruction. Identical inputs/actions/persisted state must retain identical requests, payloads, calculations, results, navigation and side effects. Preserve all current behavior including known inconsistencies. Do not change domain/backend/data/API/types/language persistence/geometry engines.

## Brand Commitments
User approved Precision Atelier direction A, Geist and Geist Mono, Porcelain and Graphite themes, mineral teal, matte surfaces and precise data-first hierarchy. No generative SaaS/card/gradient/blur decoration.

## Operating Context
Browser-first implementation and review. Seven existing routes; preserve all actual controls, nested mounting and latent-feature boundaries. No new workflow features. Light/dark remain the existing theme toggle. Bilingual behavior remains unchanged.
